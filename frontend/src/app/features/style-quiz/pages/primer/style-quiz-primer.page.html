Before You Begin

Sharp lines and soft lines

Sharp lines

Angular, elongated, bold. Think straight edges, defined angles, and a commanding presence.

Soft lines

Rounded, soft, delicate. Think gentle curves, small features, and a lush presence.

A Few Things to Keep in Mind

  • This is about your natural body lines, not your weight or size.
  • Think about your overall impression, not individual features in isolation.
  • Answer based on how you look at a healthy, comfortable weight for you.
  • There are no wrong answers. All types are beautiful.
