LYKE — Brand Pack (UX/UI)

1. Brand Essence
Brand Positioning
Confidence through relevance.
Brand Personality
Grounded. Inclusive. Intelligent. Calm.
Never preachy. Never elitist. Never trend-chasing.
Tone of Voice (In-App)

    Clear and reassuring
    No fashion snobbery
    No exaggerated claims
    No “perfect body” language

Example:
❌ “Find your dream body look”
✅ “See how this fits on people like you”

2. Colour System
Primary Colour
Midnight Green (Primary Brand)

    Hex: #0E3A3B
    RGB: 14, 58, 59

Usage:

    Primary buttons
    Navigation bars
    Key headings
    Active states

Psychology:
Trust + calm + credibility. Avoids fast-fashion brashness.
Secondary Colours
Slate Blue (Secondary UI)

    Hex: #5F7394
    Used for secondary buttons, icons, charts.

Mustard (Accent / CTA Highlight)

    Hex: #D1A72E
    Used sparingly for:
    Primary CTA emphasis
    Notifications
    Key highlights

Rule: Mustard = action or emphasis only.
Neutrals
Soft Stone (Background)

    Hex: #F4F2EE

Warm Grey (Cards / Surfaces)

    Hex: #E3E0DA

Charcoal Ink (Primary Text)

    Hex: #2A2D32

Muted Grey (Secondary Text)

    Hex: #6B6F76

Accessibility

    White on Midnight Green passes AA for headings.
    Charcoal on Soft Stone for body text.
    Never use Mustard for body copy.
    Minimum 4.5:1 contrast for text.

This app must feel inclusive — accessibility is non-negotiable.

3. Typography
Primary Typeface Recommendation:
Inter or SF Pro (clean, modern, highly readable)
Hierarchy:
H1 – 32–36px – Midnight Green – Semi-bold
H2 – 24–28px – Charcoal – Medium
Body – 16–18px – Charcoal – Regular
Caption – 14px – Muted Grey
No script fonts. No “fashion editorial” typography. This is product-first.

4. UI Component Style
Buttons
Primary Button

    Background: Midnight Green
    Text: White
    Border radius: 12–16px
    Slight shadow (very subtle)

Secondary Button

    Outline: Midnight Green
    Text: Midnight Green
    Background: Transparent

Accent Button

    Background: Mustard
    Text: Charcoal Ink

Cards (Core to Product)
Cards are central to LYKE.

    Background: Warm Grey or White
    Border radius: 16–20px
    Soft shadow (low elevation)
    Clear product tagging section

Each outfit card should include:

    Creator photo
    Body profile summary (height, size, fit note)
    Tagged items
    Save / Like function

5. Iconography
Style:

    Thin line icons
    Slightly rounded corners
    No sharp, aggressive angles

Icons should feel:

    Calm
    Friendly
    Neutral

Avoid heavy filled icon sets.
6. Imagery Guidelines
Critical for LYKE.
Imagery must:

    Be real-world, not studio-heavy
    Show diverse body types
    Include natural lighting
    Avoid heavy retouching

Content should feel:
Authentic, not aspirational fantasy.
7. Interaction Principles
UX Principles:

    Reassurance first
    Relevance immediately visible
    Minimal cognitive load
    No clutter

The app should feel:

    Calm
    Structured
    Intentional

Not like Instagram.
8. Product Feel
LYKE should feel:

    More considered than TikTok
    Less sterile than a retailer PDP
    More human than a virtual try-on tool

Design keywords:
Grounded. Clean. Modern. Inclusive. Trustworthy.
9. Microcopy Guidelines
Replace fashion hype with clarity.
Instead of:
❌ “Transform your look”
Use:
✅ “See how this fits”
Instead of:
❌ “Flawless style”
Use:
✅ “Fit notes from someone your height”
10. Logo Usage
Primary Logo:
Midnight Green on light background
Reversed:
White on Midnight Green
Clear space:
Minimum height of the “L” around the logo
Never:

    Stretch
    Add gradients
    Add shadows
    Overlay on busy imagery

12. Overall Design Goal
LYKE must feel:
Confident enough to guide decisions.
Calm enough to reduce anxiety.
Human enough to build trust.
If it ever starts looking like:

    Fast fashion chaos
    Influencer gloss
    Tech startup neon

It’s gone wrong.
